# Character balance for v5.5

This playtest changes starting Core Stats only. Every profession keeps 18 points, with each stat between 1 and 5. New preset characters now start at 15 HP, 10 Sanity, and 4 Focus; see C4_HP_HEAVY_ATTACKS_V55.md for the subsequent HP playtest adjustment. Damage, enemy stats, DCs, and encounter rules are unchanged.

The investigator focuses on observation and analysis. The ritual specialist trades physical resilience and observation for ritual knowledge and mental defense. The medium focuses on communicating with spirits and supporting teammates. The bodyguard prioritizes physical combat and mental defense. The intern remains a generalist. The driver combines physical combat with observation but struggles with rituals and spirits.

New characters use these presets. Existing characters retain their saved Core Stats and resources; no automatic save migration is performed. Legacy four-stat characters retain their existing rules. The reference document in docs/reference/characters remains the original source, while this document records the runtime playtest adjustment.

For C4 PDEF 12, BODY 5 hits on 7 or higher (70%), while BODY 1 hits on 11 or higher (50%). Damage does not increase with BODY. Against DC12, a stat of 5 succeeds 70% of the time and a stat of 1 succeeds 50% before bonuses. These are mechanical checks, not evidence of live playtest balance.
