# C4 HP pool and limited heavy attacks: v5.5 playtest

In new v5.5 no-board C4 encounters, the two ghosts divide a twelve-HP base pool scaled to the party. With 2–6 players, each ghost has 6, 9, 12, 15, or 18 HP respectively. Their total HP is 12, 18, 24, 30, or 36. Old in-progress saves retain their current HP. Other scenes and older versions retain their existing HP formulas.

Every profession gains one heavy attack per encounter. It consumes the player's one action and its one attempt even on a miss or a shield-blocked hit. It costs no Focus and grants no additional roll bonus. Damage on a hit is 3 rather than the basic attack's 2. Target defenses and the existing opening bonus remain enforced.

Zero Sanity and fear block heavy attacks. Restraint blocks physical heavy attacks but allows spirit variants. Invalid targets do not spend the attempt. Used attempts survive saves. Final armor blocks heavy damage normally; defeating the body does not stop the ritual.

New preset characters now start with 15/15 HP. Their stored maximum controls healing and ward absorption. Existing characters without a stored maximum retain the legacy ceiling of 10 without receiving automatic healing. Sanity and Focus remain 10 and 4. D4 and Final enemy HP are unchanged.
