# Encounter profession skills: v5.5 playtest

These skills are available in the v5.5 encounter without a board. They do not enable the legacy talent system. Each skill costs one Focus and the character's one action. At zero Sanity, profession skills are unavailable. Generic Focus spending for a +2 roll bonus is rejected in encounters. Other modes retain their existing rules.

| Profession | Skill | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| นักสืบ | วิเคราะห์จุดอ่อน | Opens the selected enemy to one ally attack with +2 accuracy through the next round. No roll; no extra damage; does not bypass Final armor. |
| หมอพิธี | ยันต์คุ้มภัย | Reduces the first damaging hit against one living participant, including self, by one HP or Sanity during this round's NPC phase. Does not stack. |
| ร่างทรง | สะกดเสียงกรีดร้อง | SPI DC12. On success, the selected enemy cannot declare or execute MDEF attacks during this round's NPC phase. Physical attacks remain available. Failure still spends Focus and the action. |
| บอดี้การ์ด | ยืนคุ้มกันเพื่อน | Redirects the next HP attack against one living ally to the bodyguard. The bodyguard gains the existing +2 guard defense for the round. The cover is consumed on the attack attempt. No cover chains. |
| นักศึกษาฝึกงาน | ช่วยตั้งหลัก | Removes one living ally's restraint; otherwise restores one Sanity without a roll. Recovery uses the same per-target encounter limit as the shared SOC encouragement action. |
| คนขับรถ | ลากเพื่อนพ้นอันตราย | Removes a living ally's restraint and grants the existing +2 guard defense for the round. No HP recovery or implicit change of map position. |

Temporary wards, cover, and silence expire after the NPC phase. Openings retain the existing next-round expiry. Saved encounters retain these states. Existing saves without the new fields load without migration.

In v5.5, ROOTED prevents striking and leading a team withdrawal. It also prevents the physical bodyguard and driver skills. Observation, support, and guarding remain possible. The shared BODY DC12 escape action consumes one action. Restraint retains its existing expiry through the next round; it does not remove a player's entire turn. Other participants can still lead a team retreat.

Final shield stages, ritual stopping conditions, and one-boss-turn warning for FLOOD_MEMORY remain enforced. Silencing does not win an encounter or stop the ritual. This is a playtest extension, not a replacement for the original profession reference document.
