# Ghost move variety for v5.5

This playtest extends only the no-board v5.5 encounter attack menu. Existing enemy stats, HP scaling, normal attacks, and the number of actions per enemy remain unchanged. New variants deal one HP or Sanity damage and target one participant. Older versions and board encounters retain their original move lists.

- C4 enables UNDERFLOOR_HAND as a shadow-hand variant in this authored no-board scene. It deals one HP and applies the existing restraint through the next round. DROWNED_WHISPER deals one Sanity and applies fear through the next round.
- D4 adds THREAD_SNAP, which deals one HP and breaks guard on a hit, and COERCED_VOICE, which deals one Sanity and applies fear.
- Final adds BRACELET_LASH, a one-HP guard-breaking move, and RITUAL_COMMAND, a one-Sanity fear move. FLOOD_MEMORY retains its original phase and advance-declaration requirements. None of these moves stop the ritual or restore a defeated body.

Conditions apply only on successful hits, including hits whose damage is absorbed by a ward. Guard-breaking attacks still face the target's guard for their own hit check. After a hit they remove guard and apply unsteady through the next round, preventing self-guard and the bodyguard's defensive skill.

Fear prevents spirit calming and profession skills, but does not remove the participant's entire action. Ordinary physical actions, observation, guard, and teammate support remain available unless another condition blocks them. The shared RES DC12 recovery action removes fear and unsteady without restoring Sanity. Existing SOC DC12 encouragement removes fear on success and retains its shared per-target attempt limit. The intern can remove restraint, unsteady, or fear in that priority order; otherwise the skill restores Sanity under the existing limit. The driver removes restraint and unsteady while guarding a living ally.

Silence blocks all MDEF move variants. Bodyguard cover still redirects only HP attacks, including their on-hit conditions. Temporary states survive saves and expire by round. The full move list and tactical effects are visible only to the DM; players see conditions and events that have occurred.
